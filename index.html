Curriculum & Lesson Plan

Structured curriculum designed to take students from absolute beginners to tournament-ready masters.

Phase 01

Initial Level

  • Board setup & orientation
  • Piece movement logic
  • Special moves (Castling)
  • Simple checkmate patterns
Phase 02

Beginner Level

  • Opening principles
  • Tactical motifs: Forks, Pins
  • Algebraic notation
  • Basic endgame play
Phase 03

Intermediate Level

  • Positional play concepts
  • Pawn structure evaluation
  • Advanced combinations
  • FIDE Swiss tournament prep
Phase 04

Advance Pro Level

  • Deep engine evaluation
  • Blindfold chess & viz
  • Master game preparation
  • Complex endgame theory
